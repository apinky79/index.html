# Digital business card

## The simple way (iPhone)

1. Open **`index.html`** in Safari on your phone (or run `npm run serve` on your computer and open the link on your phone).
2. Type your name, phone, email, and anything else you want.
3. Tap **Save to iPhone**.
4. Open the downloaded file → **Add to Contacts**.

You now have a digital business card on your phone. You can share it from Contacts like any other contact.

No Apple Developer account. No Terminal commands.

---

## Apple Wallet (optional, harder)

The **Wallet** app (where plane tickets live) needs a special signed file from Apple. This folder can build that, but only if you have a **Mac**, **Apple Developer membership ($99/year)**, and follow **`WALLET-SIGNING.md`**.

Most people only need **Contacts** — same details, much simpler.
