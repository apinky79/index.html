Want the card inside Apple Wallet?

Apple Wallet is the app with boarding passes and tickets. Putting a business card there is possible, but Apple does not let websites create Wallet cards directly. You need a Mac, an Apple Developer account ($99/year), and a one-time technical setup.

If you still want that, we included a builder in this folder. Read WALLET-SIGNING.md (or ask someone technical to run it for you). For most people, Contacts is enough — your details are on your phone and easy to share.
