My Season Stats — Support & Privacy

Last updated: August 30, 2025

Support

Thanks for using My Season Stats! If you need help, found a bug, or have a feature request, get in touch:

  • Email: support@myseasonstats.app

We aim to respond within 48 hours.

Privacy Policy

My Season Stats respects your privacy. This app stores your football match data (dates, opponents, goals) and any photos/videos you add locally on your device. We do not operate servers for this app and we do not collect personal data.

Information We Collect

  • No personal data is collected on our servers.
  • All teams, games, goals, and notes are saved on your device using Apple’s local storage.
  • Photos and videos you attach stay on your device.

Photos & Videos

  • Access to your Photos library is requested only when you choose media to attach.
  • Media is not uploaded anywhere unless you explicitly share it using iOS share options.
  • Backups you make with iCloud/iTunes may include your data per Apple’s settings.

Permissions

  • Photos Library — to select photos/videos to attach to a game.
  • Camera (possible future feature) — only requested if you use it.

Analytics, Tracking & Ads

We do not use analytics SDKs, third‑party tracking, or advertising networks.

Third‑Party Services

None. The app works offline and does not connect to external services in this version.

Data Retention & Deletion

  • You control your data. Delete entries anytime in the app.
  • Deleting the app removes its local data from your device. (Backups you previously made may still contain older copies.)

Children’s Privacy

My Season Stats does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If we make material changes (e.g., adding optional cloud sync), we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@myseasonstats.app.
